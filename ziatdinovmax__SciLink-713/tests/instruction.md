# Issue

**Title:** Shared replay gate, verdict record and recipe choice across the analysis agents (#712, PR A)

## Description

## Summary

PR A of #712: the three analysis agents (curve, image, hyperspectral) each judged a replay of a locked recipe, said what "verified" means and chose among a series' regime recipes in their own way, and the copies had drifted — three open issues (#710, #711, #699) are that drift seen from three angles. This PR moves those **policies** into one place, `exp_agents/_replay.py`, and routes every agent through them, **with no change in behaviour**: a parity test holds the verdict each agent now stamps to the verdict `main` reconstructed from the result's shapes, over result shapes built by the agents' own code, and requires them identical with no allow-list. The fixes themselves come in PR B, on top of this, each written once for all three agents.

Nothing changes for a person running an analysis: the same results, the same files, the same `reuse_validity`. What changes is that every result now carries its verdict where it was decided (`verdict`: verified or not, why, and who decided — the agent's QC gate, a replay gate, the recipe a follower replayed, or nobody), so the swarm board and `run_task`'s rows read it instead of reconstructing it.

## Technical description

- **`_replay.py`.** `verdict_record(verified, reason, decided_by ∈ qc_gate | replay_gate | recipe | excluded | none, …)` — one dict shape (`own_gate` kept as a derived key for the stage-2 readers; `interpretation_checked: false` reserved for PR B). Three replay gates with one verdict shape `{verdict: good | poor | failed, score, threshold, reasons, gate}`: `ScoreReplayGate(accept, threshold, metric)` for the curve's R² and the image's vision score; `FeatureHealthGate` (the image strict-replay gate, body moved here as `feature_health`); `MapReplayGate` (the hyperspectral per-map gate, body moved here as `map_health`). The controllers keep `_replay_feature_gate` / `_replay_map_gate` as aliases for their callers and tests. `select_recipe(candidates, run, judge, strategy="first_good")`: today's rule — in lock order, the first the gate calls good, else the first that executed — with `tried` on the result and `last_failed` for the strict path.
- **Routing.** Curve `qc_try_reuse` → `select_recipe` + `ScoreReplayGate` (the per-candidate folders, verbatim-first and the one correction ladder from #707 unchanged); image strict → `FeatureHealthGate`, image soft → `ScoreReplayGate`; hyperspectral per-map → `MapReplayGate`. `unit_verdict_for` returns a `verdict_record`.
- **Stamps.** `final_verdict_record(final)` is stamped by the curve and image `_compile_results` (`results["verdict"]`), by the hyperspectral cube once its status is decided (and carried in its compact `analysis_results.json`) and by the hyperspectral series when assembled; a hyperspectral series ROW is stamped (`unit_verdict`) in `build_series_row`, where everything about it is known. `analysis_verdict` reads the stamp first; `reconstructed_verdict` (the old logic, unchanged) serves results from before the stamp and is the parity reference.
- **Two reason strings** of the reconstruction were reworded to the stamped aggregator's so a reader of either path sees one text: the hyperspectral row's "not every target of the unit was approved: a of b (unit X)" (count before unit) and the series success text "every unit verified by the series driver". The `verified` bit is unchanged everywhere; these are the only edits to the old logic.
- **Tests.** `tests/test_verdict_parity.py`: curve series (11 real-path shapes through the real series and refit controllers and compile), curve single runs (8 QC-record shapes through the real compile, plus the real reuse path good and poor), image series (6), a hyperspectral cube through the real `analyze()` (strict replay, success and failure, and the saved file), hyperspectral series rows through the real row builder (7 outcomes, every combination of up to three). Each: the stamp is present, `analysis_verdict` reads it, and it equals the reconstruction exactly. The existing harnesses, board, reactions, HS and live-loop suites run unchanged apart from two assertions pinning the reworded strings and one exact-dict assertion loosened to the keys it meant (the stamp gained `decided_by`).
- **Verification.** Full suite against the `main` baseline: 24 failed / 29 errors, the same ids as `main` (3938 passed) once the one branch-only failure was taken: `tests/qc_golden/test_realtime_golden.py` pins a realtime frame's whole result, and the only change is the additive `verdict` block, so the golden was regenerated (5a1e2445; the diff is that block alone). The six other `qc_golden` failures are `main`'s own. No-swarm live regression (Bedrock Opus 4.8, plain `AnalysisOrchestratorAgent.run_task`, from a frozen snapshot, 21 of 21): a single Raman spectrum (row verified "approved by the analysis verifier", `scripts/fitting_script.py`, no `unit_verdict`, stamp = reconstruction, decided by the QC gate); the three-spectrum series (one series row, anchor stamped by its gate and both followers by the recipe, feature table and `series_fit_results.json` as before, `scripts/<unit>.py` per unit and nothing else, stamp = reconstruction); a locked-script reuse of it (R² 0.978 good, `reuse_validity` with the old keys and no `recipes_tried`, decided by the replay gate); the Ti L2,3 cube (every target approved, decided by the QC gate, the compact `analysis_results.json` carrying the same verdict); and its verbatim locked replay through the map gate (decided by the replay gate, stamp = reconstruction). No stamp failed.
- **Non-goals, as #712 states:** no base class, no change to pipelines, prompts or skills; the board, reactions and `meta_agent/` untouched apart from reading the record through `analysis_verdict` as before. The hyperspectral execution policy (#699) is PR B.
