## Technical description

### #710 and #711 (59a56cee, one commit — they share the machinery)
- **Lock time.** The curve series driver stamps `locked_recipes[regime].drift_state`: the anchor's curve as the monitor's own state (`_replay.drift_state_of`, at its own x — a curve longer than 8192 points reduced by block means, never a point interpolation), and when the series is done re-stamps each regime with its units (`_restamp_regimes` → `drift_state_of_curves`, at most 12 evenly spaced, `drift_state_units` = the curves the monitor seeded; every series shape, a single regime's untagged rows included), with `x_range` so the identity floor is the same with and without the data files. `series_recipes` carries them; the single-recipe reuse path reads them.
- **Reuse time.** `_regime_references` builds, per candidate regime, a `DriftMonitor` seeded with the regime's units' data (`spectrum_NNNN/data.npy` of the prior run; the stamp when the files are gone) and the identity reference of the units' fitted parameters. `state_distance` = `judge()["fraction"]` of the new curve against the regime. `select_recipe(strategy="nearest_first", distances=…)` tries the nearest first (unknown last, lock order among ties); ambiguity = the second within `AMBIGUITY_RATIO` (2×) of the first or both under the material bar (0.10), written to `regime_choice` (`note`), `source` (`[regime choice ambiguous: …]`) and `message`. The repair fallback repairs the nearest; a strict failure records the choice. An older run with neither data nor stamp keeps lock order.
- **The judge** (`judge(n, result)`): the GATE verdict alone decides whether a candidate is kept (ordered by the distances), then `_identity_of` records the checks — `identity_check` against the regime's units and the state distance (`SAME_STATE_BAR`, 0.25) — as flags (`identity.flagged`, `state_flag`, `state_check`, `curves_not_seeded`). `identity_features`: the names a recipe assigns (`phase`, `space_group`, … normalised, `database_id` ignored) and `(position, strength)` per component from nested or flat layouts (position words on the parameter's own name; amplitudes, widths and errors never positions; a lone flat number such as `activation_energy` is not a component). `identity_reference`: the units' strong positions (≥ `STRONG_SHARE` 10 % of the strongest) clustered by nearest neighbour, each the range the units put it in, with a floor of 1 % of the x-range; the names as sets with the unit count. `identity_check`: a name outside the set, a strong position no cluster is near, or a cluster every unit had with no strong position near it is drift; component count is not. No check changes a verdict. `reuse_validity` carries `identity`, `state_distance`, the flags and `regime_choice`. Names are compared as one string without spaces or as token sets (`names_match`: a group under its spellings; `{tio2, anatase}` ⊇ `{anatase}`; `{tio2, anatase}` vs `{tio2, rutile}` differ).
- **The record.** `interpretation_checked` only when identity is within and the state distance is under `CERTIFY_STATE_BAR` (0.10; `SAME_STATE_BAR` 0.25 is the flag bar) — against one reference unit too (`interpretation_checked_by`); a series follower carries `regime_checks` from `_check_follower` (the anchor's stamped curve, what the anchor found) and is certified by them; `final_verdict_record` says False for `qc_gate` runs, for a fresh cube, and for series whose units are not all checked; a hyperspectral replay's required maps held to the anchor's statistics counts; a series whose ANCHOR replayed a prior recipe is `replay_gate` (followers replaying the series' own anchor are its mechanics). `analysis_verdict` passes `decided_by` and `interpretation_checked` beside the verdict; the board's `_claim_verified` posts a replay's claims as provisional unless the interpretation was checked, the recipe stays verified. CLAUDE.md states the boundary and why the fingerprint was dropped.
- **Tests.** `tests/test_regime_choice.py` and `tests/test_identity_check.py` as described in the round-1 reply, including the reviewer's 5/5/6/6/9-peak anchors and the real reuse path end to end.
